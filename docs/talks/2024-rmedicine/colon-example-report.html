Colon recurrence report

In a clinical trial, patients were randomized to receive adjuvant chemotherapy with either levamisole, levamisole + 5-FU, or placebo. Interest was in whether treatment group was associated with time to recurrence.

Time to recurrence was measured from the date of treatment start to the date of recurrence or last known date the patient was recurrence-free. The Kaplan-Meier method was used to estimate recurrence-free probabilities. Multivariable Cox regression adjusted for age and sex.

Characteristic 5-year RFS (95% CI)
Treatment
    Obs 45% (40%, 51%)
    Lev 46% (41%, 52%)
    Lev+5FU 62% (56%, 67%)

The 5-year (95% CI) recurrence-free probability is 45% (40%, 51%), 46% (41%, 52%), and 62% (56%, 67%) for the placebo, levamisole, and levamisole + 5-FU groups, respectively.
